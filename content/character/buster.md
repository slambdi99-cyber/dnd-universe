Met the party when he came with Tavin to the Peapod Pub to pick up the cask of Misenchanted Lavender Mead. He stayed, shared some drinks, spilled some secrets, and bonded closely with Tobias.

## Early life

He has recounted facing racial discrimination from a young age, when children threw rocks at him and belittled his tusks.
