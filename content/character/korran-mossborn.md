Born not among his people's peaks but in the lowland marshes, after a rockslide swept his tribe away during a seasonal migration. Found as a child by a wandering hermit of the Bogwatchers' Sanctum and raised in their hidden temple amid the mists and moss.

Taught to channel power without aggression and strike only when harmony was broken. He earned the name the Still Hand for staying calm when violence loomed.

Works behind the bar at the Peapod Public House, listening. He unknowingly served the party Maera's amnesia-inducing Misenchanted Lavender Mead.

He drinks little and speaks less, and occasionally disappears for a few days, returning with bruised knuckles and bog water on his robes.
