Owner of the Peapod Public House and Korran's previous boss. Tobias Goreguts' session log spells her Myra.

## Controversy

Under investigation by the Enchanters' Guild for the Misenchanted Lavender Mead.

She had been receiving financial support from the Underbelly Mercantile in exchange for letting them use her inn as a safehouse on occasion. That relationship gave her access to exotic alchemical ingredients, specifically Memory Lattice, which she wanted to use to conjure wonderful memories during Lucian's first night of residency.

Her experiments worked. The finished mead was then altered by the oak cask, and instead of reinforcing memory it destroyed it.

So the amnesia was an accident of cooperage, arranged by a woman trying to do something kind for a bard who was murdered at her pub a few months later.
