Melda Vaelorith is Wren's aunt and a political leader of House Vaelorith. Decrepit but sharp, she handles matters the House would rather keep off the record, moving through Lorithal's politics with the composure of someone used to being obeyed.



A letter from her was found on Seamus Stonebuckle's body. Her trail first crossed the party during the Enchanters' Exchange, including some suspicious smithing antics in Valeshire, and she remains tied to House Vaelorith's interests there.
