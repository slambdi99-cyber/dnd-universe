The Buried Star grants the desires of those it favours, through psychic wish magic. Wren holds a piece of it.

She also reported a voice in the back of her head when a gemstone touched a root in the Covenant's chamber, and the session log notes she has a lot of information about the Covenant that she has not shared. Those facts sit together uncomfortably.
