The Dissenters are a grassroots resistance movement in Lorithal, opposed to the city's current royalty. They have no cells, no hierarchy and no figurehead. Membership is marked by hanging coloured cloth, which is closer to flying a flag than to joining a cause.

## Names and cries

**The Dissenters** is the canonical public-facing label.

Their cries are *"Truth to Lorithal!"* and *"For the Fallen Heir!"* — the second is a slogan, not the movement's name, and not a faction in its own right.

## Their claim

That the Day of the Fallen Heir marks a concentration of magical power in Lorithal's upper echelon, and that the fallen heir was killed by House Vaelorith, the family's power usurped by conspirators.

## Escalation

Signs of the movement gathering force, in rough order:

- Colour creep, cloth appearing where white should hang
- Dyed water sent down the tier spillways
- A House banner pulled down mid-crowd
- A martyr named in chalk
- Bells tolling out of turn
- The Kindling faltering, as pockets of the crowd refuse to look up
