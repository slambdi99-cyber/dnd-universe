A few dozen members, mostly elves — business leaders in masonry, excavation, finance, and land. Their gaudy fashions are presented as haute couture and worn as signals: the right cut, the right orchid, the right scent says "I'm in the game."

Membership isn't sworn. It's whoever gets the joke, wears the signals, and gets invited back.
