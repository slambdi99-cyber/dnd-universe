The Abyssal Brood are a tribe of blue kobolds living in the cave systems around the Everpool, beneath the Dire Foothills of Copper Ridge. They are worshippers of Broodmaster Goluub, and a serious threat to anyone venturing into the region.

## Culture and society

They are hostile to outsiders in their territory, and prefer secrecy and harmony with the land, as their Broodmaster instructs. They pay attention to how their habits affect the ecosystem around them.

Goluub has installed a firm social hierarchy. Adult females hunt and fish for the tribe's food. Young females forage for berries, herbs and mushrooms. Adult males mine raw material for smelting. Young males maintain the tunnels and help process ore.

## Beliefs

There is no clergy. Every kobold in the tribe is an aspirant of the Broodmaster.

They believe they are continually reincarnated within the brood, along with the souls of the animals they hunt and the invaders they kill. A misbehaving kobold is understood to be the first incarnation of an enemy's soul, and later lifetimes are expected to see them integrated more respectfully.

Ritual sacrifice is a tradition of devotion. A pregnant progenitress may select a progeny to be reborn from her egg; that kobold is placed under Goluub's control, collared with a chain of lead, and walks into the Everpool. Enslaved kobolds acting as Vessels of Goluub conduct the ceremony.

They say the souls of the Brood wait at the top of a great cliff, and once they have learned how best to serve the tribe in their next life, they leap from the edge and fall back into the ocean of the mortal plane.
