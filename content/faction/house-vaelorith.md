House Vaelorith is the ruling elven house of Lorithal. Its seat is a triple-spired hall of dark stone at the city's northern edge, the tallest structure in Lorithal, though its spires peek over the top tier rather than looming over it.

## The family

- **Melda Vaelorith** — Wren's aunt and a political leader of the House, who handles the matters it would rather keep off the record.
- **Seren Vaelorith** — Wren's brother and Melda's nephew, a House contact for magical reference books in Lorithal.
- **Wren** — of the Vaelorith line, and secretive about her connection to the city.

## The Kindling

The Kindling, also spoken of as the Day of the Fallen Heir, is Lorithal's annual public rite and is held under the House's authority.

## Opposition

The Dissenters ride under the cry "For the Fallen Heir!", holding that the House's claim is illegitimate and that a rightful heir was set aside long ago.
