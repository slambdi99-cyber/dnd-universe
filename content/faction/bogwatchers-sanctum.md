They keep a hidden temple amid mists and moss, and teach that every drop of water, every root and every whisper of wind carries a lesson.

Their seers have sensed corruption spreading through the nearby towns: deforestation, poisoned waters, and greed. Given what mining at Copper Ridge has done to the region's water, the Sanctum's interests and the vale's collapse are the same story.
