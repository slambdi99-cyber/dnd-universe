## Copper Vale hierarchy

- **Regional Operator**: Dak Patterson, always accompanied by an entourage
- **Consorts**: Darius Vell, Worrick Thistleby, Seamus Stonebuckle
- **Underlings**: Buster, Tavin, and others

Whatever sits above the regional tier is unrecorded.

They fund Maera Broadkettle's pub in exchange for using it as an occasional safehouse, contract Foreman Rumbleshot for ore, and ultimately profit from the Rumbleshot Quarry. The party are members of the Belt, a secret policing group keeping tabs on them, and have killed one consort already.
