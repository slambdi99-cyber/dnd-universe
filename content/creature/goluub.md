The Dire Foothills kobolds call him Broodmaster. He inhabits the Everpool.

Kobolds represented him with a doll. Kasbor resisted him and helped the party. Found at the bottom of an elevator beneath the kobold warren, in a huge cavern like an amphitheater around an underground lake.
