The working district — trades, salvage, and the rougher end of the city's supply chain. Four establishments anchor it: Bryth Surveying Supply for adventuring and digging gear, Krantz Salvage for scrap and things without receipts, Ashe & Sons Ironworks for metalwork, and The Wet Boot for the cheapest beer in the city.
