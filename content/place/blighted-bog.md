Standing water with nowhere to go, appeared within living memory as the
lowlands stopped draining. Not an old marsh.

The regional map labels it the Shallow Bog, the Brindlewood area map calls it
Willow Bog, and the party earned the name Blighted Bog the hard way. One bog,
three names.

The Underground Chamber lies beneath it, reached through a perfectly round
puddle in a circular clearing.
