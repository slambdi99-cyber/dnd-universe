Not an old marsh. It is standing water with nowhere to go, and it appeared within living memory.

The Underground Chamber lies beneath it, reached through a perfectly round puddle in a circular clearing.

The DM's wiki places the chamber under the Shallow Bog, which strongly suggests the Blighted Bog of the relationship graph is this same water under a later name. Not merged yet, pending confirmation.
