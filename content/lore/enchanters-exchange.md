The Enchanters' Exchange brings several guild chapters together for instruction, demonstration, and rivalry.

## Chapters

**Valeshire** students show that tradesmen and merchants can also be wizards.

**Dawnmere**, north of the copper hills, sends reserved scholars of patience and virtue.

**Ashcroft**, south of Arrowfell, sends determined and militant students.

**Ironfeld**, west of the foothills, is known more for metalworking than for magic.

**Lorithal**, east of the Wayward Steppes and within the kingdom of House Vaelorith, sends skilled elves.

## Lessons

### Day 1

Barnaby Thistlewick has the students introduce themselves by choosing a leader and demonstrating the skill that best embodies their chapter. While they prepare, he works with the teaching assistants and lays out the class's culminating project.

The students are brought inside, and the team leaders put their skills on display.

- Dawnmere: Seraveth demonstrates Religion, childish but glowing with the Morninglord's holy light.
- Ashcroft: Darvek demonstrates Athletics, tossing a peer into the air.
- Ironfeld: a student demonstrates Arcana with artistry, transmuting rock.
- Lorithal: Faeron demonstrates Medicine by trying to embarrass Wren with prestidigitation on her hair dye.

The party plans to ask Seren for magical reference books on vampires, the Gulthias Tree, and Goluub.

### Day 2

The party leaves for Lorithal. Barnaby is left to manage the Exchange.
