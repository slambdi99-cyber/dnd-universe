The Kindling is one of Lorithal's major public rites, also spoken of as the Day of the Fallen Heir. The party arrived in Lorithal on the eve of the celebration and witnessed the city gather beneath a reactive magical field.

During the rite, orange-white wisps spread across the sky, bells sent ripples through the barrier, and the crowd's attention visibly gathered into shared points of light overhead. Ashbright University contributed public magical displays, including arcane fireworks and illusions.

The rite is presented publicly as a celebration of Lorithal's traditions and shared future.

Elaric appeared during the Kindling, to the party's surprise. Wren recognized him in the middle of the public spectacle and suffered a severe panic attack, fainting as the rite unfolded around her.
